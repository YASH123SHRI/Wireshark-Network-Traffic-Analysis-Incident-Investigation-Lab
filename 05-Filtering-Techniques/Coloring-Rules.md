# Wireshark Coloring Rules

## Objective

Configure and use Wireshark coloring rules to visually identify specific types of network traffic during packet analysis.

---

## Background

Coloring rules allow analysts to visually distinguish packets based on protocol, packet characteristics, or custom display filters.

They can make large packet captures easier to review and help draw attention to potentially important traffic.

---

## Default Coloring Rules

Wireshark includes predefined coloring rules for common packet types.

Examples include:

- TCP
- UDP
- ICMP
- HTTP
- DNS
- Errors and unusual TCP conditions
